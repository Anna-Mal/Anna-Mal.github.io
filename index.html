Skills & Expertise

Statistical & Data Analysis

Analysis-ready data and interpretable study results

Study analysis: prepare and quality-check datasets from observational studies and randomised controlled trials; co-develop Statistical Analysis Plans.

Longitudinal outcomes: use regression and linear mixed-effects models to analyse associations and changes over time while accounting for repeated measurements and confounding.

Complex biological patterns: combine exploratory analysis, ordination and differential abundance testing to investigate variation in high-dimensional data.

Interpretation: account for confounding and multiple testing, and explain findings in the context of the study design and biological question.

Communication:create publication-quality figures and R Markdown reports that make longitudinal trends, group differences and analytical choices easier to review.

Multi-omics & Biomedical Data

Connecting microbial composition and function with health outcomes

Microbiome profiles: characterise taxonomic composition, diversity and functional potential from 16S rRNA and shotgun metagenomics data using tools including MetaPhlAn4 and HUMAnN3.

Protein-level evidence: analyse MaxQuant and FragPipe outputs through protein annotation, differential expression and functional enrichment to interpret metaproteomic findings.

Nutrition and metabolism: combine dietary intake records with continuous glucose monitoring to quantify postprandial responses and investigate differences between participants.

Data integration: link microbiome and metaproteomic profiles with clinical, nutritional and experimental metadata to support interpretation across data types and repeated measurements.
